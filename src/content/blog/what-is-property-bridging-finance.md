When a property is sold in South Africa the seller usually only receives the proceeds once the
transfer is registered in the Deeds Office. That process typically takes 60 to 90 days, and in the
meantime the seller may need money to pay outstanding rates and levies, to secure the next property
or simply to keep cash flowing.

Property bridging finance closes that gap. Reliance Trading SA advances up to 80% of the surplus
proceeds that are due to you on registration. The conveyancing attorney then settles the advance
from those proceeds when the transfer goes through.

## Who uses bridging finance?

- **Sellers** of residential and commercial property who need access to their equity before transfer.
- **Estate agents** who want their commission before registration.
- **Bond holders** with an approved new or further bond waiting to register.
- **Buyers** who need to pay transfer duty and costs before their bond proceeds become available.
- **Businesses** that need a short-term secured loan when bank facilities are exhausted.

## Is it a loan?

No. The agreement is a discounting agreement: we purchase, and take cession of, your claim to the
surplus proceeds. Our right to payment only arises on the registration date. See our
[FAQs](/faqs/) for the details, or [contact us](/contact-us/) to discuss your transaction.
